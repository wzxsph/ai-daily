## [Nvidia to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-snaps-up-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion-as-closed-ai-labs-pull-away/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Nvidia is acquiring open-source AI platform Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, roughly 80 times its reported $150 million in annual revenue. The deal aligns with Nvidia&\#x27;s broader push to invest billions in open AI models at a time when closed AI labs such as OpenAI and Anthropic are increasingly moving away from Nvidia hardware. The acquisition would give Nvidia direct control over the most influential open-source AI hub, strengthening its position in the AI ecosystem as closed labs seek hardware independence. It also signals that Nvidia views open-source models as strategically critical to maintaining influence over the AI stack, even at an extreme revenue multiple. The 80x revenue multiple strongly suggests a strategic rather than purely financial rationale, as such premiums are rarely justified by current cash flows. Hugging Face hosts a vast repository of open models, datasets, and tools, and had previously expanded into robotics with its acquisition of Pollen Robotics in April 2025.

**Background**: Hugging Face is a leading platform for open-source AI, serving as a central hub where developers share, discover, and download pre-trained models and datasets, most notably those built on frameworks like Meta&\#x27;s NLLB. Open-source AI models have rapidly closed the quality gap with proprietary systems—trailing frontier closed models by only about 3 percentage points on major benchmarks—but still capture just 4% of global AI revenue. Meanwhile, closed AI labs like OpenAI and Anthropic have been developing custom silicon and diversifying hardware suppliers, reducing their reliance on Nvidia GPUs and motivating Nvidia to deepen its ties with the open-source community.
